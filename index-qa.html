About Cerniam

Built for What Retail Becomes

A New Foundation for Retail Operations

Cerniam was founded on a simple observation: the retail industry generates enormous amounts of data, but most of it never becomes insight. Legacy systems silo information, creating blind spots that cost billions annually.

We built Cerniam to be the connective tissue between every system, channel, and decision in retail. Our platform unifies fragmented data into a single intelligence layer that learns, adapts, and acts at machine speed with human oversight.

Backed by a team of retail operators, data scientists, and systems engineers, Cerniam serves mid-market and enterprise retailers across North America and Europe.
